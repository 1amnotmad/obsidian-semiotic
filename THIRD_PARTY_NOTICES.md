## Semiotic Standard symbols (`src/icons/`, embedded in `theme.css`)

The Semiotic Standard iconography was designed by Ron Cobb for *Alien* (1979).

The SVG files are vector adaptations by **LouH** ([github.com/louh/semiotic-standard](https://github.com/louh/semiotic-standard)). They are based on icon recreations by [Brandon Gamm](https://thenounproject.com/gamm/collection/semiotic-standard-icons-from-alien/) and licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The copies used here come from [banastas/SemioticStandard.org](https://github.com/banastas/SemioticStandard.org), which optimised them for the web. The build step minifies them and URL-encodes them as data URIs. The geometry is unchanged.

The CC BY 4.0 licence covers the vector adaptations only. It does not grant rights to the original film artwork, names, or trademarks.
